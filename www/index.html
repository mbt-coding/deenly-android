Time and calendar

Hijri dates use the calculated Umm al-Qura calendar. Official months start by moon-sighting announcements, so your country/mosque may differ by ±1 day — use the correction field.

Weather units

Weather comes from Open-Meteo for your selected location and needs internet. The last result is kept so it can still be shown offline.

Adhaan and prayer reminders

All Adhaan

Pick the reciter for every prayer except Fajr. A reciter can only be selected after you add a real recording of that reciter. Files you add are stored on this device and play offline; links need internet. Deenly doesn't include recordings and can't check who is reciting.

Fajr Adhaan

Fajr has its own choices. Use a genuine Fajr Adhaan recording (it includes “As-salatu khayrun minan-nawm”); a regular Adhaan should not be used as the Fajr Adhaan. Without a Fajr recording, Fajr plays a short chime.

Long beep

A sustained tone made by the app. It is not an Adhaan.

Device alarms

Alarms that work while the app is closed need the Deenly Android app: it schedules them with the system alarm manager for the next 7 days and re-arms them after a restart. Battery savers and some phone makers (for example Xiaomi, Huawei, Oppo, Samsung) can still delay or block alarms, so allow Deenly to run in the background. Open the app every few days so new days are scheduled. Test it on your own phone before relying on it for Fajr.

Offline Packages

Quran Reader

Arabic text (Uthmani), translations and transliteration come from alquran.cloud and need internet; Surahs you open are saved on this device for offline reading. Recitation: Mishary Rashid Alafasy, streamed from Islamic Network (not stored in the app). Tap a verse number to mark it as your last-read position.

Qibla Finder

The bearing is calculated from your selected location (degrees clockwise from true north). Without the compass, the arrow is relative to north-up. Compass readings can be off — calibrate by moving the phone in a figure 8, and keep away from metal.
